## Replacing them

Keep them unbranded. The licence covers the photograph and not a character someone else owns: a
photograph of a Spider-Man figure carries Marvel's rights and the sculptor's as well, and this is
marketing imagery on a public repository.

**Look at any replacement rather than trusting its description.** Of six candidates chosen by alt
text for this set, four were branded — Buzz Lightyear, two Stormtroopers, Woody and an Ant-Man. The
Ant-Man photograph was described as having "no identifiable franchise".

Crop to 4:5 portrait; the cards use that frame and anything else is cropped to fit. Around 900px
wide at quality 80 is enough for a card that renders about 300px across, and keeps the four of them
near 400 KB in total.
